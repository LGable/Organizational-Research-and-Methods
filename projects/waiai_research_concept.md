# Proposed Research: Workplace Accommodation Implementation Adequacy & Burnout

**Status: Unpublished doctoral concept / developmental research methods. Not an empirical study.**

## Question

Among employed autistic and/or ADHD healthcare workers who report a functional workplace support need, is opportunity-specific implementation adequacy during Weeks 0–8 associated with personal exhaustion at Week 16, conditional on baseline burnout, workload and organizational context?

## Proposed design

- Prospective observational cohort, three-wave architecture across approximately 16 weeks.
- Baseline and Week-16 burnout conceptually assessed using an authorized six-item personal Copenhagen Burnout Inventory scale; do **not** reproduce copyrighted instrument items or infer licensing permissions here.
- Candidate WAIAI differentiates support availability from provision at the needed opportunity and actual practical usability.
- Baseline job demands, managerial resources and context would be adjustment variables.
- Employee exit, missingness and people with no relevant opportunities are measurement issues, not automatically failures.

## Theory

Relational Person–Environment Fit is the lead conceptual frame; Double Empathy is contextual rather than causal proof, with Job Demands–Resources and Conservation of Resources as supporting perspectives.

## Limitations

The need for a new index, its score validity, its incremental value versus established constructs, and the proposed longitudinal association remain **unproven hypotheses**. Ethics approval, accessibility research, pilot testing and formal psychometrics are pending.
